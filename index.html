Meet Maria

A Little Café That Feels Like Home

Sunshine Cafe is Maria's kitchen — every soup, sauce, loaf of bread and pastry is her own recipe, made fresh in-house.

Guests come for the lentil soup and tagine, and come back for the welcome. Maria will tell you what's in each dish, bring you a little extra to try and treat you like family. It's a sunny spot to grab breakfast before the park, meet a friend over coffee or settle in to study.
