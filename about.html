Meet Maria

Her Own Recipes, Her Own Bread

Maria is the owner, the cook and the baker. The soups, sauces, pastries and even the bread are all her own recipes, made fresh in the café.

She cooks with Moroccan roots and a big heart. Guests say she makes them feel like their grandmother is taking care of them — explaining every dish, bringing fresh bread and little extras, and making sure no one leaves hungry.

Sunshine Cafe sits on 21st Street, a short walk from Astoria Park — the perfect stop before or after a stroll.

Our Philosophy

We believe in homemade food, fresh ingredients and treating every guest who walks in like family.
